# Blender 5.3 headless pipeline: verified behaviour

Verified 2026-10-27 with Blender 5.3.0 Beta (`/Applications/Blender.app`, Python 3.13) while building `art/animals.py` and `art/island.py`.

- **CLI arguments**: `blender -b --python script.py -- a b` leaves Blender's own flags in `sys.argv`; scripts must read what follows `--` (`rig.script_args()`). Before this fix, the procedural scripts took `art/<script>.py` as their output path under the CLI.
- **Exit codes**: add `--python-exit-code 1`, otherwise a Python exception still exits 0 and `set -e` scripts carry on.
- **FBX import changes the frame rate** to the file's 24 fps. Clips keyed at 30 fps then export 25 % slower (an idle of 60 frames became 2.5 s). Restore `scene.render.fps` after importing.
- **`Image.save_render(path, scene=sc)` uses the scene's image settings**, so setting `file_format = 'JPEG'` for it leaks into later renders (a `.png` path then gets `0007.jpg` appended). Restore the settings afterwards.
- **Automatic weights** (`parent_set(type='ARMATURE_AUTO')`) work headless and take a fraction of a second on 5–6k-vertex meshes, but leave small separate pieces unweighted (74 bunny vertices) and let arms modelled against the body drag the torso. See the rules in `art/rig.py` (`Skin`).
- **glTF export of several clips**: `export_animation_mode='ACTIONS'` writes every action as a glTF animation named after the action; with `export_force_sampling` all 27 nodes get T/R/S channels per clip. Actions need `use_fake_user` to survive.
- **Bone axes survive export**: in three.js each bone's local +Y still points along the bone (checked on `chest` and `head`).
- **Multi-material skinned meshes** export as one node whose primitives become `name_1`, `name_2`… SkinnedMesh children in three.js.
- The Khronos validator (`gltf-transform validate`) reports one warning on Blender's skinned exports, `NODE_SKINNED_MESH_NON_ROOT` (the mesh is a child of the armature node); three.js renders them correctly.
- **`gltf-transform optimize --compress meshopt`** (v4.5) keeps skins, all animations and their durations; the textured characters (11 clips each) go from 1.4–1.6 MB to 530–660 KB.
- **Saving a `.blend` after `pack_all()`** also packs images only referenced by orphaned materials (the FBX's 4096² texture); purge orphans first (`bpy.data.orphans_purge(do_recursive=True)`).
- **`scene.ray_cast`** on the evaluated depsgraph is a reliable way to measure a bumpy ground surface; percentile filtering of upward-facing face centres was skewed by seats and benches. It also tells what a loose piece stands on (cast down from just inside its bottom, skip hits on itself): face-centre proximity failed on the big ground triangles of low-poly islands.
- **gltf-transform `optimize` prunes empty leaf nodes** (its `prune` step, `keepLeaves` off): empties exported as anchors vanish unless `--prune false` is passed (`art/build.sh` does for `landing_island.glb`). Empties with children survive.
- **`export_animation_mode`**: in Blender 5.3 `'SCENE'` wrote one glTF animation per animated object (named after the object); `'ACTIVE_ACTIONS'` ("active actions merged") gives the single clip a scene loop wants. Shape keys export as morph targets with `export_morph=True`; three.js plays them through `morphTargetInfluences` (negative values swing the other way).
- **Workbench previews ignore node colours**: with `color_type = 'TEXTURE'` an untextured material shows `material.diffuse_color` (the viewport colour), not the Principled base colour; copy one into the other before rendering a preview of procedural parts.
- **Pose bones in armature space**: after `view_layer.update()`, `pose_bone.matrix` is current; a world-space rotation `q` becomes a local delta `M⁻¹ q M` (M = the bone's current armature orientation), which is how `Anim.aim`, the two-bone IK `Anim.reach` and `Anim.level` (a bone back to its rest orientation, e.g. a foot flat on the floor) work. Verified by measuring the sole points of the generated clips: planted soles move at a constant speed to 3 decimals.
- **Mesh repairs with bmesh**: `holes_fill` + `poke` closes holes with fans (no sliver triangles, which make heat weighting fail); integer face and vertex attributes (`part`, `island0`) survive `bmesh.ops.duplicate`, `delete` and `to_mesh` round trips, so a cut's pieces can be traced afterwards. Automatic weights fail ("Bone Heat Weighting: failed to find solution") when small enclosed pieces sit inside the body; retrying without them works (`rig.heat_bind`).
- **`rlib.mat()` caches materials across `rig.reset()`** (only `rlib.reset()` clears `_MATS`): a script that builds several scenes in one run (`art/kitchens.py`) must clear `rlib._MATS` after each reset or it gets `StructRNA of type Material has been removed` on the second scene.
- **gltf-transform `optimize` quantizes** (verified 2026-10-28 on `kitchen.glb`): each mesh node of the shipped file carries a scale (e.g. 0.25 for a 0.5 m box) and an offset that dequantize its int16 positions. JS that sets such a node's `scale` or `position` outright undoes that (the fryer's oil box became 2 m wide); add to the node's existing values instead. Empties (template roots) keep scale 1 and are safe to move and scale.
- **Levelling a tilted import**: a plane `z = ax + by + c` fitted by least squares through the bottoms of counter-sized pieces (two outlier passes) and `Vector((-a, -b, 1)).normalized().rotation_difference(Vector((0, 0, 1)))` applied to the mesh data put the three cooking dioramas flat to within 2 cm.
