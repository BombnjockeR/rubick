# Development log

Significant milestones, decisions and open issues only. Newest first.

## 2026-10-27 — Landing island and the new landing page

- **Landing island** (`art/landing_island.py` → `assets/landing_island.glb`, 1.26 MB): the AI-generated "floating island game" FBX (`art/source/environments/landingpage/`) cleaned up and completed by script. Removed: the broken plaza statue (a spike of stretched faces), two overlapping blimps with loose fins, a mooring rope, the bare windmill pole, cube "islets". Added: rock undersides (the islands were open shells), a fountain with a gold cat holding a racket, an airship with a spinning propeller, a windmill, mini islets, nets, pennant strings, clouds, lobbing balls, steam. One 10 s baked loop (bobbing landmass, islets, balloons, airship; propeller, sails, balls, steam); wind is procedural in the page. Pieces are classified by rule (components, texture colour, ray casts), not by hand-picked ids.
- **Landing page** rebuilt as a full-screen 3D stage (owner request, modelled on HKDI's "Design Island"): drag to turn, pinch or wheel to zoom, pins on the three games, the plaza (how it works), the entrance bridge (host), the airship (watch from afar) and the clock tower (good to know); each opens a floating glass panel and flies the camera closer. Headline card with the host button and the join-by-code form; dock of chips; loading screen with an intro dolly. Phones get a bottom sheet and the island framed above the cards. `assets/img/hero*.jpg` and `og.jpg` are stills of the new scene; `tennis.jpg` and `tabletennis.jpg` were re-captured from the current games (island court, animals).
- **Pipeline**: `build.sh landing` / `npm run assets:landing`; `rlib.Geo` gained `poly()` and `prim()` for hand-built faces; gltf-transform's pruning is off for this asset (it dropped the empty anchor nodes).
- **Verified** in headless Chrome: the page at 1440×900 and 390×844 with no console errors or failed requests, 61 fps on an Apple M5, all seven pins placed and the focus flight to each; `npm run smoke` passes 49/49. **Not verified**: real phones (touch drag, pinch, the bottom sheet), Safari and Firefox, slower GPUs.

## 2026-10-27 — Natural tennis animation, lineup choice, AI demo

Owner request: fix whatever looks odd or unnatural, remodelling if needed (future characters will come in a T-pose); let the host force-start and watch the AI; let players choose 1v1, 2v2 and so on.

- **Models**: both animals had the left hand fused to the body (bunny: on the hip; pup: buried in the shorts pocket). The build now frees it: the bunny's forearm is cut off the body, the pup's forearm and fist are rebuilt by mirroring the right arm; both are turned forward and out and that becomes the rest pose. The cut is cleaned by connectivity (no loose shards), and the holes are patched in the colour of the clothing around them.
- **Clips** (11): new `ready` (two-handed ready position, left hand on the racket throat by IK), `strafeL/R` (side steps), `serve` (overhead, trophy pose first), `defeat`. `walk` and `run` are regenerated by foot placement (IK) so planted feet don't slide; the forehand's follow-through and recovery no longer pass the racket across the face; the defeat head-drop is milder.
- **Character system**: `setVelocity` (sideways moves play the side step), `setStance('ready')`, an upper-body layer for `swing` and `serve`, gait speeds set to the clips' measured stride speeds, walk/run switch with hysteresis (a steady mix of the two slid).
- **Racket engine**: the animals now stand so the ball arrives on their racket hand (it arrived on the left, the backhand side, because the chibis hold their racket on the other side); ready stance during play; side steps for short moves, a run only for dashes the side step can't keep up with; Tennis serves overhead (`serveClip`) from where the toss is (the ball used to jump to the contact point); the losers play `defeat`.
- **Waiting room**: × / + on empty seats (keys 1–4) for any lineup with a player on each side; *Start now* / `F` starts at once, or runs a repeating AI-only demo when nobody has joined (`F` leaves it).
- **Tools**: the character viewer gained stance, sideways speed, *Move the character* over a floor grid, serve and defeat; the smoke test measures the planted sole while walking, running and side-stepping, and covers the waiting room and the defeat at match end.
- **Testing**: `npm run smoke` 49/49 and `--full` 51/51 (both games played to the end: winners `victory`, losers `defeat`); planted-sole slide, measured on the skinned shoe vertices: walk 2 %, run 2–3 %, side step 8–14 % of the distance travelled (before: walk and run slid at close to the body's speed). Close-up renders of every new pose and of the remodelled arms for both animals; AI-demo screenshots of serve, rallies and hits in both games at ~61 fps.
- **Not verified**: real phones and motion controls with the new timing of the serve animation; other browsers and slower GPUs/TVs.

## 2026-10-27 — Animal characters and the island court

- **Characters**: the Bunny and the Pup (AI-generated FBX in `art/source/`, unrigged) are rigged, skinned and animated entirely by script (`art/rig.py`, `art/clips.py`, `art/animals.py`) into `assets/bunny.glb` and `assets/dog.glb`: 22-bone animal skeleton with ears, tail and accessory sockets; clips idle, walk, run, jump, victory, swing; the modelled racket split into a tool mesh, plus a generated table-tennis paddle.
- **Character system** `js/character.js` (`MabarChars`): configurable definitions, skeleton-safe cloning, speed-driven idle/walk/run blend, full-body one-shots with cross-fades, upper-body swing/ready layer, tools, accessory sockets, outfits, graceful load failure.
- **Games** (owner decision): Tennis uses the animals on the island court (`art/island.py` → `assets/island_court.glb`; the stadium is now the fallback venue); Table Tennis uses the same animals with paddles in the existing hall (camera raised to 3.5 m, animals 1.55 m tall so they don't hide the table). Point winners hop, match winners play victory. Cooking is unchanged. No message-format or networking change.
- **Pipeline**: `art/build.sh` takes targets and runs the installed Blender app headless (`npm run assets:animals`); the procedural scripts now read their arguments after `--`. `Kit3D.load` resolves URLs against `document.baseURI`.
- **Testing**: `npm run smoke` (headless Chrome via the DevTools protocol, no dependencies) passed 34/34, and 36/36 with `--full` (both games played to the victory animation): both characters' clips, skinning, tools, speed blend and cross-fades, jump, victory, swing, transforms, sockets, a missing-file error; Tennis and Table Tennis played to the first point at ~61 fps on an Apple M5; fallback to chibis and the stadium with the new files blocked; landing, host (every game), controller and watch pages without console errors. Also run: split screen and doubles with simulated players, `gltf-transform validate` (no errors), and rig/animation review renders in Blender.
- **Not verified**: real phones and motion controls with the new characters; other browsers (Safari, Firefox) and slower GPUs/TVs; the remote-screen video showing the animals (the watch page loads, the stream path wasn't exercised).

## Open issues

- No automated tests, linting or CI; regressions are caught only by manual play.
- `npm run assets` (all targets) would regenerate the procedural `.glb` files with Blender 5.3; they were exported to a temp dir to check the scripts run, but not regenerated or compared.
- Animation limits of the source models: the short arms barely reach above the head, so the trophy pose and overhead serve are stylized; the remodelled left arms still show small patched seams close up (the pup's hip, where its buried forearm was removed), and the bunny's left sleeve creases when that arm crosses the body; the free arms were cut from AI-generated sculpts, and a T-pose model would avoid all of this.
- Feet still slide slightly above ~4.6 m/s (Tennis dashes reach 5.3 m/s, 7 m/s in doubles; the run plays at most 2.6× speed so it doesn't look frantic) and during the ~0.2 s walk ↔ run cross-fade.
- The island's stands, trees and props are the AI model's own low-poly geometry; only its court, net and the ground under the new court were replaced.
- During long headless test runs the public PeerJS broker dropped the host's socket twice (of ~12 runs); the hub reconnected by itself, and a side-by-side run of the previous commit behaved the same.
- `art/source/` and `art/reference/` are not backed up by git.
- Landing island: a few painted pennant strips fused into the source landmass were left (they read as streamers); the gold cat is a primitive sculpt; the hole the statue left in the plaza is only covered by the fountain basin.
- GitHub repo is still named `rubick`.
