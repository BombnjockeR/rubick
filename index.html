Host a game Runs right here in this browser

To host, open this page on a laptop or TV browser and choose Host a game. Phones are the controllers.

Three steps to a party

  1. 1

    Open it on the big screen

    A laptop works, or a laptop plugged into the TV. Click Host a game.

  2. 2

    Pick a game, share the QR

    Each game opens with a lobby and a QR code. Friends point their phone camera at it.

  3. 3

    Play with your phone

    Swing it like a racket or use it as a gamepad. Empty seats are filled by AI players.

What you can play

Tennis

Your player runs to the ball. You decide when to swing and how hard: swing early to send it left, late to send it right. Real scoring, from 15–love to advantage.

Swing your phone1 to 4 playersSingles or doubles

Table Tennis

Short, fast rallies across the table. The timing ring tells you when the ball is in reach. With two of you, the screen splits so each side gets its own view.

Swing your phone1 to 4 playersFirst to 5

Cooking

Chop, cook, plate and serve together before the orders run out. Soups burn if you forget them, and somebody always does.

Phone gamepad1 to 4 chefsCo-op

Good to know

Nothing to install
Everything runs in the browser. Friends only need a phone with a camera to scan the code.
Any network
Phones don’t have to share your Wi-Fi. Mobile data works too.
A friend in another city
They open the watch page on their own laptop or iPad to see your game live, then join with their phone.
On iPhone
Tap Allow when asked about motion. That’s how your swings are detected.

Mabar comes from main bareng: playing together.
