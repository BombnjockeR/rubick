How it works

Three steps to a party

  1. Open it on the big screen. A laptop works, or a laptop plugged into the TV. Click Host a game.
  2. Pick a game, share the QR. Each lobby shows a QR code and a room code. Friends point their phone camera at it.
  3. Play with your phone. Swing it like a racket or use it as a gamepad. AI fills the empty seats.
